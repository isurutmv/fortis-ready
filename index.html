The expensive mistake

Teams book a $20k–$80k audit, then burn weeks and budget on findings that a focused readiness pass would have caught earlier — missing tests, unclear scope, broken access control, upgrade patterns nobody documented.

What audits are for

Deep, formal review by a firm you trust — when capital and users are on the line. That process deserves clean code, clear docs, and a defined surface.

What readiness is for

Catch the obvious and structural issues first. Ship tested, scoped, documented contracts so your auditor starts on real risk — not homework.

FAQ

Is this a security audit?

No. It is a fixed-scope engineering readiness review. It does not replace a formal audit from a reputable firm when you need one.

What if my contract is larger than ~300 nSLOC?

SKU-1 is for a single tightly scoped surface. Larger or multi-contract systems fit SKU-2 (Audit Prep Sprint) or a custom quote.

Do you take custody of keys or funds?

Never. We review code you share (repo / commit). Zero custody is part of the mission.

What do I get in the report?

Severity-rated findings, PoC notes where useful, remediation order, and notes on tests/docs for auditor handoff. See the sample report in our product materials.

How do I pay?

USDC on Base to the address listed at intake confirmation. 0x2415e94be90b4785f4b79f49db067c89925cc1ca.

Can you guarantee my contracts are safe?

No. No honest reviewer can. We help you reduce obvious and structural risk before audit and deployment — you own residual risk.
